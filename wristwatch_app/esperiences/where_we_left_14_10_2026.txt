SESSION STATUS & WHERE WE LEFT OFF: OCTOBER 14, 2026
================================================================================
Date: October 14, 2026
Target Hardware: Waveshare ESP32-C6FH8 with 1.9" ST7789 LCD (320x170 Landscape) + QMI8658 IMU
Mobile Companion: Flutter App (`wristwatch_app`) on Android 14+ (Poco X7 Pro / HyperOS)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. COMPLETED & ADOPTED FOR FINAL PRODUCT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Real-Time Volume Sync (< 15ms Instant):
   - Fully solved & verified. Removed blocking `Serial.printf` inside NimBLE callbacks
     (eliminated 1000ms USB CDC timeout stall), added `Serial.setTxTimeoutMs(0)`,
     and made `currentVolume` volatile. Syncs instantly across both app slider and
     physical rocker. (Documented in `esperiences/volume_sync.txt`).

2. Messenger Notification Hijack Prevention:
   - Fixed WhatsApp/messaging avatars hijacking cover art by decoupling `event.largeIcon`
     from player identification and establishing strict whitelist/blacklist filters.
   - Added cover art length deduplication to avoid redundant BLE retransmissions.

3. 100% Lossless QOI Cover Art Streaming (<100ms Total Latency):
   - **CONFIRMED & ADOPTED AS FINAL PRODUCTION STANDARD.**
   - Tested and verified: Visual quality is crystal clear, 100% lossless, with zero
     JPEG blockiness or banding.
   - Reduced payload from 28.8 KB (raw RGB565) to ~8-14 KB QOI.
   - High-speed 480-byte BLE chunks at 3ms pacing = ~70-90 ms wire transmission.
   - Embedded native C++ decoder on ESP32-C6 RISC-V decodes directly to `artBuffer`
     in ~2 ms with ZERO dynamic heap allocations.
   - Replaced and removed external `JPEGDEC` library dependency completely.

4. Pop-up Notifications & Phone Call Alert Modals (BB05 Protocol):
   - Implemented transient modal overlays on top of persistent media UI.
   - Messages (WhatsApp / SMS): Floating rounded card (280x124 px at X:20, Y:23) with
     app badge, sender name, 3-line word-wrapped message body, and a real-time
     countdown timer bar that shrinks over 6 seconds before auto-dismissing.
   - Phone Calls: Full-screen takeover alert (320x170 px) with pulsating red banner,
     prominent caller name, number/status, and visual Decline / Accept action pills.
   - Screen State Machine: Zero frame buffer memory overhead; automatically restores
     full media player screen in < 15 ms upon modal dismissal or call end.
   - Android Routing: Fully handles incoming phone calls, WhatsApp messages, and SMS,
     with test buttons in the companion app UI for instant display verification.
   - Documented in `esperiences/pop_up_notifications.txt`.
